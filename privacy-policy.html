Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026  ·  Developer: XoRdbX Studio

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games and applications published by XoRdbX Studio that link to this page from their store listing or from within the application.

XoRdbX Studio publishes several games and applications. Depending on the Game, we may use Google services such as Firebase, Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Only the services actually integrated into the Game you use apply to you.

1 Who We Are

The games and applications covered by this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Games”) are developed and published by XoRdbX Studio, a sole proprietorship (Entreprise Individuelle) registered in France under RCS Valenciennes, SIREN 994 303 238.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use one of our Games, why it may be processed, which third-party services may be involved, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

2 Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to any XoRdbX Studio Game that links to this page. Different Games may use different features, SDKs or monetization models.

Therefore, a section concerning a particular service applies only when that service is actually integrated into the Game you are using.

If a Game introduces a new category of data processing or a new third-party service that is not covered here, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

3 Data We May Process

Our Games do not intentionally require users to provide their name, postal address, telephone number or other directly identifying information unless a specific feature clearly requests it.

Depending on the Game and the services it uses, the following information may be processed:

  • Firebase Analytics, where used: app-instance identifiers, session information, device and operating-system information, language, approximate country or region, and gameplay events such as level starts, completions, feature use or other in-game interactions.
  • Firebase database services, where used: technical or gameplay telemetry such as a game/grid identifier, game result, words or actions played, duration, timestamps or similar non-account gameplay data.
  • Google AdMob, where used: advertising-related information such as ad impressions, ad interactions, basic device information and, where legally permitted, advertising identifiers.
  • Technical and security information: limited diagnostic, integrity, performance or abuse-prevention information necessary to operate and protect the Game and its services.

4 Purposes of Processing

  • Provide the Game and its core functionality.
  • Improve gameplay, difficulty, balancing, usability and performance.
  • Measure usage and understand which features are used.
  • Display advertising in Games that are supported by ads.
  • Detect errors, fraud or abuse and protect our services.

5 Legal Bases and Consent

Where applicable data-protection law requires consent, we ask for consent before the relevant processing begins.

  • Consent: may be used for analytics storage, personalized advertising and related advertising purposes where required by law.
  • Legitimate interests or service necessity: may apply to limited processing needed to provide, secure, maintain or troubleshoot a Game, where permitted by applicable law.

The exact legal basis can depend on your location, the Game you use and the feature or service involved.

6 Google UMP and Privacy Choices

Games using Google AdMob may use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another Google-compliant consent mechanism to present privacy choices in regions where this is required.

Where configured, consent signals may also be used through Google consent mode for advertising and analytics purposes.

If a Game provides a Privacy, Privacy Options or Ad Options menu, you can use it to review or change the choices available for that Game.

7 Advertising

Some Games may be free and supported by advertising through Google AdMob.

Advertising may be personalized only where this is legally permitted and, where required, after valid consent. Otherwise, contextual or non-personalized advertising may be shown, subject to applicable law and platform rules.

8 Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Depending on the Game, information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Game, including:

  • Google Firebase — Google Privacy Policy · Firebase Privacy & Security
  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy · AdMob Privacy & Consent

These providers process data according to their own terms, privacy notices and applicable data-processing agreements.

9 International Transfers

Some service providers may process data outside your country, including in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses may be used for such transfers.

10 Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to the retention settings and policies of the relevant service provider.

Analytics, advertising and technical data may have different retention periods. Where possible, data may also be aggregated or anonymized.

11 Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • Access to personal data concerning you
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure of data in applicable circumstances
  • Restriction of or objection to certain processing
  • Data portability where applicable
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time for future processing based on consent

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In France, the supervisory authority is the CNIL.

12 Children's Privacy

Some XoRdbX Studio Games may be intended for a general or mixed audience, which may include children.

For any Game declared as targeting children on Google Play, we apply the additional requirements relevant to that audience. In particular, children and users of unknown age must not be served interest-based advertising or remarketing, and advertising SDKs and ad formats must comply with the applicable Google Play Families requirements.

Where a Game has a mixed audience and age-based treatment is required, an appropriate neutral age-screening mechanism may be used before enabling age-dependent advertising or data-processing features.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal data inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

13 Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14 Your Controls

  • In-app privacy controls: where available, use the Game's privacy or ad-options screen to review available consent choices.
  • Device controls: Android and other platforms provide privacy, advertising and permission settings that you can manage directly.
  • Contact us: you may contact XoRdbX Studio regarding a privacy request relating to one of our Games.
